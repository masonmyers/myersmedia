Mason Myers

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Mason Myers is the Founder and CEO of Myers Media, where he also serves as the head of the Myers Media executive committee. Mason’s vision for Myers Media is to be a true creative haven. We are intentionally structured to prioritize art and empower creators, giving them the support to innovate without a corporate agenda. While other major players focus on market trends, we focus on talent.

Joel Myers

General Counsel

Joel Myers is General Counsel and a member of the Myers Media’s executive committee, overseeing the company’s comprehensive legal landscape. He is directly responsible for managing all corporate contracts, business transactions and litigation, ensuring the company and its artists are protected. Joel brings decades of high-level experience in litigation, IP, and business law, making him a formidable advocate for Myers Media.
