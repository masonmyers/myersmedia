Services

At Myers Media, we offer various services centered around artists and their creations

Content Creation and Live Production

We provide a full-spectrum content creation service built around the artist's creative vision, encompassing professional music production and comprehensive film production. Our studio-side capabilities handle every technical detail, from initial song composition, advanced mixing, and mastering for all digital formats, to deep pre-production tasks like script development, storyboarding, casting, and location scouting for film projects. We ensure the captured content—whether audio or visual—is produced using industry-leading equipment and techniques, offering technical direction and sound design that preserves the unique artistic voice while adhering to the highest global quality standards.

Extending our creative reach beyond the studio, we are leaders in the development and execution of dynamic live entertainment services. This includes the meticulous planning and staging of concerts, brand activations, and large-scale public events. Our team manages all logistical complexities, including venue coordination, technical staging, audio-visual (AV) design, and talent management for live performances. By integrating creative direction with operational expertise, we guarantee a seamless, high-impact final product, ensuring that the live experience is a cohesive and memorable extension of the artist's recorded work.

Rights Management and Commercial Strategy

Our business services are anchored by expert music licensing and rights management, which is crucial for capitalizing on intellectual property. We handle all complexities associated with both the sound recording (Master) and the musical composition (Publishing), securing the necessary synchronization, mechanical, and public performance licenses needed for content usage worldwide. This involves rigorous ownership registration with collection societies and digital platforms, constant use tracking, and proactive claiming to prevent unauthorized distribution and revenue diversion. We treat IP protection not as a defensive measure, but as a proactive strategy to generate and manage various royalty streams for our creators.

To complete the commercial lifecycle, we provide strategic promotional advertising services tailored specifically for media and entertainment content. This involves designing and executing targeted marketing campaigns across digital and traditional channels, public relations, and influencer outreach to effectively build an audience and drive consumption metrics. By aligning marketing spend with precise rights tracking, we ensure every promotion converts into verifiable streams and sales. This strategic alignment, combined with our ability to streamline content delivery to all major DSPs, guarantees our artists the clearest path to successful distribution and maximum monetization.
