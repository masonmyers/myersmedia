Leadership

The talented team of visionaries guiding Myers Media to new heights.

Mason Myers

Mason Myers is the Founder and CEO of Myers Media, LLC where he also serves as the head of the Myers Media executive committee. Mason founded Myers Media to be the change he wanted to see in the media world. After observing an industry dominated by legacy conglomerates—where short-term profits and shareholder demands often override artistic integrity, he knew a new model was necessary. Mason’s vision for Myers Media is to be a true creative haven. We are intentionally structured to prioritize art and empower creators, giving them the support to innovate without a corporate agenda. While other major players focus on market trends, we focus on talent. We provide our artists with premier resources and a dedicated team, ensuring their work is championed with the passion and long-term vision it deserves. Our foundation isn't shareholder value; it's the value of creation.

Joel Myers

Joel Myers is General Counsel and a member of the Myers Media’s executive committee, overseeing the company’s comprehensive legal landscape. He is directly responsible for managing all corporate contracts, business transactions and litigation, ensuring the company and its artists are protected. Joel brings decades of high-level experience in litigation and intellectual property law, making him a formidable advocate for Myers Media. Joel graduated with a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Georgia Institute of Technology and holds a J.D. from University of South Carolina School of Law. His expertise is built on this strong academic foundation from GT and USC School of Law. At USC, he served as a student instructor for Legal Writing and Oral Advocacy and was the founder and president of the USC Intellectual Property Law Society. Joel is also a strong advocate for community service and giving back to the less fortunate. He has served as a Big Brother in The Big Brothers Big Sisters program for more than 3 years.
