Family owned and operated

Bringing an exhilarating change to the media industry

Myers Media LLC is a newly founded, family-owned media company dedicated to dismantling the entrenched oligopoly in music ownership and rights. Led by Son and Father duo, Mason Myers and Joel Myers, the company rejects traditional industry standards that prioritize corporate and executive profits over artistic livelihoods. Myers Media's core mission is a radical commitment to securing sustainable ownership and livelihood for all artists and creators.

The company’s mission is to be artist-first, challenging established giants whose dated models focus solely on near-term shareholder profit. Myers Media is driven to implement radical transparency in royalty accounting and distribution, fundamentally changing the economics of music to create a more sustainable long-term value. Our goal is to put power and revenue back into the hands of creators, granting them greater control and a significantly higher share of their work’s value, which we believe is the truest path to success for us, our creators, and our investors.

How Myers Media is commited in giving back to the community

Services provided at Myers Media
