Giving Back to the community

How Myers Media is committed to giving back

Community Investment and Talent Pipeline

Our corporate commitment begins by maximizing impact through our most accessible resources: our expertise and network. To cultivate the next generation of creative talent, we are focusing on providing essential services and establishing immediate mentorship initiatives. Furthermore, we actively champion employee-driven giving by implementing a donation matching program that doubles the philanthropic impact of our staff’s personal contributions to eligible arts and cultural non-profits.

As our company grows, we are laying the foundation for a dedicated, long-term Arts and Media Education Grant. This fund will launch its initial cycle within the next two years, offering direct financial assistance at local educational organizations. The grant's focus will be specific and impactful, targeting programs that introduce digital media production, music technology, and licensing education to underserved high school and college students. In the interim, Myers Media is establishing a comprehensive mentorship initiative where our team of industry experts in music production, marketing, and legal affairs will partner directly with local high schools and arts organizations to teach crucial professional skills, including digital rights and content monetization strategies while also conducting various workshops.

Local Engagement and Partnerships

To demonstrate our commitment in the community, we will establish a dedicated Pro Bono Content Creation Initiative, dedicating a set percentage of our promotional advertising capacity to create marketing assets for local non-profit organizations focused on arts education or community betterment. This uses our creative strength to amplify meaningful social impact. The Myers Media Strategy ensures that our business operations directly stimulate the local economy while leveraging our media platform to uplift community partners and causes.

Myers Media is here to support more than just the art. We commit to dedicating part of our revenue directly to mental health non-profits, ensuring vital resources are provided to artists and the community. It's our way of making sure everyone has the solid support they need to be their best self.
